BNI Sekuritas | Jan–Dec 2025 | IT Business Analyst

BNI Sekuritas needed to expand BIONS—their flagship platform for stocks, mutual funds, and bonds—to handle increasing retail investor demand while meeting strict OJK and IDX regulatory standards. Existing requirements processes caused 30% delays in feature delivery, and back-office teams struggled with manual reconciliation consuming 4+ hours daily.

🎯 Actions

  • Conducted 20+ stakeholder workshops across Trading, Risk, and Compliance teams
  • Documented 150+ user stories in JIRA with acceptance criteria linked to OJK/IDX mandates
  • Created comprehensive FRD/TSD using gap analysis to map As-Is vs. To-Be workflows

📊 Results

30% Faster Delivery
30% Cut Manual Work

In highly regulated domains like securities trading, traceability isn't optional. Every user story must explicitly map to regulatory controls. SQL validation scripts became our "compliance firewall," preventing issues before development.

🌾 KoltiPay x Artajasa: Farmer Lending Transformation

Koltiva (Agritech) | Nov 2022–Dec 2024 | Lead Business Analyst

📌 Challenge

Koltiva's KoltiPay digital wallet needed integration with Artajasa (Indonesia's national payment gateway) to enable farmers and cooperatives to access instant lending. The existing manual process required 3-5 days for loan disbursement, creating cash flow bottlenecks during critical planting seasons.

🎯 Actions

  • Led discovery phase to define MVP scope across Product, Finance, and Field Operations
  • Created detailed BRD outlining API integration requirements and transaction flows
  • Documented 200+ user stories covering wallet top-up, loan disbursement, and settlements
  • Orchestrated UAT with 10 pilot cooperatives, achieving 98% success rate

📊 Results

40% Cost Reduction
96% Faster Disbursement
98% UAT Success
35% Fewer Failures

💡 Key Takeaway

Field validation beats theoretical design. Our initial API error handling assumed stable internet—farmers' rural connectivity forced us to add offline queuing. Piloting with cooperatives early saved weeks of rework.
