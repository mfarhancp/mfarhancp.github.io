🌾 KoltiPay x Artajasa: Farmer Lending Transformation

KOLTIVA (Series B Agritech Startup) | Nov 2022 – Dec 2024 | Lead Business Analyst

📌 Challenge

KOLTIVA's KoltiPay digital wallet needed integration with Artajasa (Indonesia's national payment gateway) to enable 15,000+ farmers and cooperatives to access instant lending. The existing manual process required 3-5 days for loan disbursement, creating critical cash flow bottlenecks during planting seasons when timing is everything.

🎯 Actions

  • Led 15+ discovery workshops to define MVP scope across Product, Finance, and Field Operations teams
  • Created detailed BRD outlining API integration requirements, transaction flows, and error handling scenarios
  • Documented 200+ user stories across 6 Scrum sprints covering wallet top-up, loan disbursement, settlement, and reconciliation
  • Designed data flow diagrams showing KoltiPay ↔ Artajasa message exchanges with offline queuing for rural connectivity
  • Orchestrated UAT with 10 pilot cooperatives representing different use cases, achieving 98% first-time go-live success rate
  • Developed training modules and field guides for 50+ field officers with limited tech literacy, including offline troubleshooting
  • Implemented monitoring dashboard to track transaction failures, reducing resolution time from 24 hours to 4 hours
